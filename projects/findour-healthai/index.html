🧠 Risk Intelligence
Prototype readmission model · risk stratification · held-out performance · patient-level explainability
🧠 Risk scores are produced by a logistic regression model trained in your browser on the currently loaded dataset, evaluated with a held-out train/test split. The prototype is a 30-Day Readmission Risk Assessment at Discharge — it uses information available by the discharge decision point, not at admission. The target readmitted is never used as an input feature. Scores are decision support for human review — not a diagnosis, and not a treatment decision.
